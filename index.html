Two Sum Variant

Find two elements whose sum equals the target.

Example

Input: [2, 7, 11, 15], target = 9

Output: [0, 1]

Explanation

The numbers 2 and 7 add up to 9. Their positions in the array are 0 and 1.

Constraints

  • Array contains at least two elements.
  • Each element is an integer.
  • There is one valid answer.

Sample Test Cases

Test 1: [2,7,11,15], target 9

Expected: [0,1]

Test 2: [3,2,4], target 6

Expected: [1,2]
